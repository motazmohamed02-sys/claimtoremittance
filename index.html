Claim XML to Remittance Converter (Per-Activity Net/Payment Edit)

Browser-Based Tool
• After upload, a table appears showing Net and a Payment Amount input field for each Activity.
• **Smart Conversion:** If Payment Amount == Net for an Activity, the **DenialCode** will be removed; otherwise, it remains.
• The tool adjusts **TransactionDate** to the next day, minus 4 hours, and ensures a **unique PaymentReference**.
